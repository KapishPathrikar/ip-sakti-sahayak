# CGPDTM Open House Helpdesk Portal and Grievance Redressal Guidelines

## Office of the Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (CGPDTM / IP India)
### Government of India, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, DPIIT

---

### Section 1: Overview and Objective of the Open House Helpdesk Portal
The Open House Helpdesk Portal (https://iprsearch.ipindia.gov.in/openhousehelpdesk/Login/login) is an official interactive grievance redressal and stakeholder support mechanism established by the Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (CGPDTM).

The objective of the Open House initiative is to:
1. Provide swift, transparent, and direct resolution to stakeholder queries and procedural grievances relating to Intellectual Property rights administered by IP India (Patents, Designs, Trade Marks, Geographical Indications, and Copyright).
2. Facilitate direct virtual interaction between applicants, patent attorneys, IP agents, and senior officials of the Patent and Trademark Offices.
3. Bridge administrative bottlenecks, address technical e-filing issues, and expedite delayed or stalled IP applications.

---

### Section 2: Daily Virtual Open House Sessions and Schedule
1. Frequency and Timings: The CGPDTM conducts regular virtual Open House interactive sessions on all working days from 10:30 AM to 11:30 AM.
2. Platform and Access: The daily Open House is held virtually via platforms like Cisco Webex or designated video conferencing links published on ipindia.gov.in.
3. Attendance: Open to patent applicants, trademark proprietors, registered patent and trademark agents, attorneys, MSMEs, startups, and academic researchers.
4. On-the-Spot Grievance Redressal: During the 10:30 AM to 11:30 AM window, stakeholders can raise application-specific delays, procedural hurdles, or technical errors directly before the Controller General or designated senior supervisory officers for immediate instructions.

---

### Section 3: Grievance Submission Procedure via Open House Portal
1. User Registration:
   - Any applicant or authorized agent having an issue related to IP must register on the Open House Helpdesk Portal using a valid email address.
   - For queries or grievances related to an already existing patent, trademark, or design application, the user must register using the exact email address associated with the IP application in official office records to maintain security and authentication.
2. Ticket Creation and Categorization:
   - Users submit grievances by creating a Ticket on the portal.
   - Categories include: E-filing Errors, Examination Delays, Hearing Notice Issues, Fee & Payment / CBR Reconciliation, Certificate Issuance, and Miscellaneous Inquiries.
3. Payment and CBR Issues:
   - If the issue pertains to fee payment or Cash Book Receipt (CBR) generation, the ticket must provide:
     - Transaction ID / Reference Number
     - Temporary Application Number
     - User ID
     - Exact date and time of payment.
4. Urgent E-Filing Issues:
   - If the matter relates to time-critical e-filing errors (e.g. deadline expiring on the same day), the user should select the E-filing category and provide their direct contact phone number in the Remarks box to enable immediate phone contact by the technical team.

---

### Section 4: Response Timelines and Ticket Resolution Windows
1. Standard Turnaround and Initial Response Time:
   - Grievance tickets raised on the Open House portal are acknowledged instantly with a unique Ticket Tracking Number.
   - Technical and e-filing issues are typically assigned and addressed by the IPO IT cell within 24 to 48 working hours.
   - Procedural and administrative application queries (such as allocation for examination, issuance of First Examination Report FER, or scheduling hearings) are routed to the concerned section and updated within 3 to 7 working days.
2. Auto-Resolution Rule (7-Day Non-Response Inactivity Window):
   - Once the IPO officer or Helpdesk provides a clarification, resolution, or request for additional documents, the ticket status changes to Pending User Response.
   - If no response or confirmation is provided by the applicant/user within seven (7) calendar days of the IPO reply, the grievance ticket is resolved and closed automatically by the system.
   - The user retains the option to submit feedback or re-open the ticket if the underlying grievance remains unsatisfied.
3. Overarching DARPG Grievance Redressal Norm (30-Day Maximum Limit):
   - Under the Central Government guidelines issued by the Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances (DARPG) and the IPO Citizen's Charter, all formal public grievances must be completely resolved within a maximum time limit of thirty (30) days from the date of receipt.
   - If an inquiry involves complex inter-branch review or policy decision, an interim reply explaining the cause of delay must be communicated to the applicant within the 30-day window.

---

### Section 5: Escalation Matrix and Application Status Tracking
1. Direct Application Status Tracking:
   - Before raising a duplicate grievance ticket, applicants are encouraged to check live dynamic records:
     - Patents: https://iprsearch.ipindia.gov.in/PublicSearch/PublicationSearch/ApplicationStatus
     - Designs: https://search.ipindia.gov.in/DesignApplicationStatus
     - Trademarks: https://tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in/eregister/
     - Geographical Indications: https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublic
2. Escalation to Central Public Grievance Portals:
   - If a grievance submitted on the Open House portal remains unattended or exceeds the stipulated timeline, stakeholders can escalate the matter to:
     - The CPGRAMS portal (pgportal.gov.in) under the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT).
     - Direct representation during the daily 10:30 AM to 11:30 AM virtual Open House session citing the Open House Ticket Number.
