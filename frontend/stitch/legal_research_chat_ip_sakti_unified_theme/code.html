What are the patent exclusions for traditional medicine?

policy

Section 3(p) TKDL Exclusions

Under the Indian Patents Act, 1970, inventions that constitute traditional knowledge or aggregate known properties of traditionally known components are strictly excluded from patentability.

"an invention which in effect, is traditional knowledge or which is an aggregation or duplication of known properties of traditionally known component or components."

— Section 3(p), Patents Act 1970
